The liver is a large organ in the upper right side of your belly.

It filters blood coming from the digestive system and helps remove harmful chemicals.

The liver makes bile, a greenish fluid that helps break down fats in food.

It stores energy as glycogen and releases it when your body needs fuel.

The liver also helps make proteins that clot blood and carry nutrients.

Even though it works hard every day, the liver can repair itself better than most organs.

A healthy liver likes water, colorful fruits and vegetables, and not too much sugary drinks.
