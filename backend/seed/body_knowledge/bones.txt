Bones form the skeleton, the strong frame that shapes and supports your body.

Adults usually have 206 bones. Babies are born with more bones that fuse as they grow.

Bones protect important organs. The skull protects the brain. The rib cage protects the heart and lungs.

Bones work with muscles and joints so you can walk, jump, and pick things up.

Bone marrow inside some bones makes blood cells.

Calcium and vitamin D help bones stay strong. Milk, yogurt, leafy greens, and sunlight can help.

Exercise like running, jumping, and playing sports tells bones to grow denser and stronger.
