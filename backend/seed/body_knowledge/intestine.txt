The intestines are long tubes in your abdomen where digestion finishes and nutrients enter the blood.

The small intestine is longer and absorbs most vitamins, minerals, sugars, fats, and proteins.

Tiny finger-like villi line the small intestine and give it a huge surface for absorption.

The large intestine, also called the colon, absorbs water and forms leftover waste into stool.

Trillions of helpful microbes live in the intestines and support digestion and health.

Fiber from fruits, vegetables, and whole grains feeds those microbes and helps waste move smoothly.

Chewing food well and drinking water make the intestinal journey easier.
