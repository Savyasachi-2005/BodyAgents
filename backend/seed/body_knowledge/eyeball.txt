The eye is a sensory organ that turns light into signals your brain understands as vision.

Light first passes through the clear cornea, then through the pupil in the center of the iris.

The lens focuses the light onto the retina at the back of the eye.

Special cells in the retina called rods and cones detect brightness and color.

The optic nerve carries those signals from the retina to the brain.

Tears keep the eye moist and help wash away dust.

Protecting your eyes with good lighting, outdoor play, and careful screen habits supports healthy vision.
