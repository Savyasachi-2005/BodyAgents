The digestive system breaks food into nutrients your body can use for energy and growth.

Digestion starts in the mouth. Teeth chew food and saliva begins to break it down.

Food travels down the esophagus to the stomach, where acids and enzymes turn it into a soupy mix.

The small intestine absorbs most nutrients into the blood. The liver and pancreas send helpful juices to the small intestine.

The large intestine absorbs water and forms leftover waste into stool.

Fiber from fruits, vegetables, and whole grains helps digestion stay regular.

Drinking water and chewing food well help the digestive journey go smoothly.
