The lungs help you breathe. They take in oxygen and send out carbon dioxide.

Air travels through the nose or mouth, down the windpipe (trachea), into the bronchi, and into tiny air sacs called alveoli.

Alveoli are like tiny balloons. Oxygen moves from alveoli into the blood, and carbon dioxide moves from blood into the alveoli to be exhaled.

The diaphragm is a muscle under the lungs. When it moves down, air flows in. When it moves up, air flows out.

Exercise makes lungs stronger and helps the body use oxygen better.

Smoke and dirty air can hurt the lungs, so clean air and not smoking keep them healthy.

You have two lungs. The left lung is a little smaller to leave room for the heart.
