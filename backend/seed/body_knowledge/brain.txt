The brain is the control center of the body. It helps you think, learn, remember, feel emotions, and move your muscles.

The brain has about 86 billion nerve cells called neurons. Neurons send tiny electrical signals to share information.

The cerebrum is the largest part of the brain. It helps with thinking, language, and voluntary movement.

The cerebellum sits at the back of the brain and helps with balance and coordination, like riding a bike.

The brainstem connects the brain to the spinal cord. It controls automatic jobs like breathing and heartbeat.

Sleep helps the brain store memories and clean up waste chemicals made during the day.

Protect your brain by wearing a helmet for sports and bikes, and by getting enough sleep and exercise.
