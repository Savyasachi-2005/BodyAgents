Skin is the body's largest organ and covers you from head to toe.

It protects against germs, injury, and too much water loss.

Skin has layers: the outer epidermis, the thicker dermis, and deeper fat that cushions and warms.

Nerve endings in skin let you feel touch, pressure, temperature, and pain.

Sweat glands help cool the body when you get hot.

Melanin pigment helps protect skin from strong sunlight.

Washing gently, using sunscreen outdoors, and drinking water help keep skin healthy.
