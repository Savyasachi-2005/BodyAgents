The heart is a strong muscle that pumps blood through the body every minute of every day.

Blood carries oxygen and nutrients to cells and carries away waste like carbon dioxide.

The heart has four chambers: two atria on top and two ventricles on the bottom.

The right side of the heart sends blood to the lungs. The left side sends oxygen-rich blood to the body.

Valves inside the heart act like one-way doors so blood flows in the correct direction.

A healthy heart likes exercise, fruits and vegetables, water, and enough rest.

Your pulse is the beat of blood pushed by the heart. You can feel it in your wrist or neck.
