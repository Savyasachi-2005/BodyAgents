The kidneys are two bean-shaped organs on either side of the spine below the ribs.

They filter blood and remove waste chemicals and extra water as urine.

Each kidney contains about a million tiny filters called nephrons.

Nephrons decide what to keep, such as useful salts and water, and what to send out as waste.

Kidneys also help control blood pressure and balance the body's chemistry.

Urine leaves each kidney through a tube called a ureter and is stored in the bladder.

Drinking enough water and eating a balanced diet help kidneys do their cleaning work.
